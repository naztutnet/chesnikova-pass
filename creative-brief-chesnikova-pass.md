# Creative brief — CHESNIKOVA PASS

## Project context

- Working title: CHESNIKOVA PASS interface direction.
- Product: Telegram Mini App for coordinators creating guest passes in PassOffice.
- Deliverables: updated working prototype and a comparison board with five single-family typography variants.
- Concept: a compact operational desk, not a cinematic badge or a corporate dashboard.
- Audience: recurring internal users who value speed, certainty, and readable status over brand theatre.

## Visual direction

- Subject: visitor requests, dates, destinations, status, and one primary action.
- Composition: 390 px mobile-first; clear hierarchy; compact rows; no oversized empty hero.
- Palette: graphite, warm off-white, neutral gray, one cobalt signal.
- Typography: one Cyrillic-capable sans family across all roles; hierarchy comes from weight, size, and spacing.
- Tone: calm, precise, contemporary, service-oriented.

## Reference logic

- Official visitor products establish the workflow: pre-register, notify, verify, track.
- Working dashboards establish the density: time/state tabs, visitor rows, clear create action.
- Pinterest/boarding-pass references are reserved for a future issued-pass or QR screen, not the application form.

## Avoid

- Standalone AP/CP logo marks at this stage.
- Serif/sans mixing.
- Film strips, gates, tickets, clapperboards, or decorative access symbols.
- Large KPI dashboards, desktop sidebars, and color on every card.
- Unverified “success” without PassOffice ID and status.

## Open decision

- Final family: System UI, Inter, Manrope, Onest, or Golos Text. Onest is the current recommendation because it is compact, readable at small sizes, supports Cyrillic, and retains more character than a system stack.
